# Things we shpuld never automate

Artificial Intelligence can be powerful, but movements must prioritise **relationships, trust, consent, and security** over efficiency. These are ten practices I will never delegate to machines.

---

## 1. Automated replies to community messages

**What AI can do:**  
Bots can send instant replies to direct messages based on keywords, helping campaigns or fundraisers “scale” responses.  

**Why I will not use it:**  
When someone from our community reaches out—especially in a moment of crisis—they deserve a human response. Automated replies feel transactional and dismissive.  

**Movement principle:**  
Relationships matter more than efficiency.

---

## 2. Sentiment analysis to monitor organisers
**What AI can do:**  
AI can track activists’ posts, flag “negative sentiment,” and measure engagement.  

**Why I will not use it:**  
This is surveillance technology. Governments use it to target and suppress protest organisers.  

**Movement principle:**  
Never surveil your own people.

---

## 3. Voice cloning to amplify organisers
**What AI can do:**  
AI can generate podcasts, audio clips, or multilingual voiceovers using an organiser’s cloned voice.  

**Why I will not use it:**  
Even with consent, cloning removes bodily autonomy. Consent must be ongoing, not a one-time agreement.  

**Movement principle:**  
Consent is continuous.

---

## 4. Automated apologies or accountability statements
**What AI can do:**  
AI can draft polished apology statements instantly.  

**Why I will not use it:**  
Apologies are about accountability. Outsourcing them undermines sincerity. If you cannot write it yourself, you are not ready to apologise.  

**Movement principle:**  
Accountability requires effort—do the work.

---

## 5. Facial recognition to tag participants
**What AI can do:**  
AI can identify faces in photos and suggest tags.  

**Why I will not use it:**  
This technology can expose organisers to law enforcement and surveillance.  

**Movement principle:**  
Security outweighs convenience.

---

## 6. Machine translation without human review
**What AI can do:**  
AI can translate content into over 100 languages instantly.  

**Why I will not use it:**  
AI often mistranslates cultural context, tone, and political nuance. Misrepresentation can harm movements.  

**Movement principle:**  
Hire human translators from the communities you seek to reach.

---

## 7. Automated comments to boost engagement
**What AI can do:**  
Bots can post supportive comments like “🔥” or “Solidarity!” on allied campaigns.  

**Why I will not use it:**  
Movements are not brands. Authentic engagement matters. Automated comments are spam, even if well-intentioned.  

**Movement principle:**  
Show up authentically for partners and allies.

---

## 8. Predictive analytics to identify “high-risk” organisers
**What AI can do:**  
AI can analyse social media behaviour to predict who might organise protests or become leaders.  

**Why I will not use it:**  
This mirrors predictive policing, which disproportionately targets Black, Brown, and Indigenous organisers.  

**Movement principle:**  
Do not weaponise data against your own people.

---

## 9. Deepfake videos to amplify campaigns
**What AI can do:**  
AI can generate videos of people saying things they never said.  

**Why I will not use it:**  
Even with consent, deepfakes erode trust. Once normalised, they cannot be controlled.  

**Movement principle:**  
Trust is everything—never gamble with it.

---

## 10. Scraping community data without consent
**What AI can do:**  
AI can harvest names, emails, and locations from public social media profiles.  

**Why I will not use it:**  
Public data does not equal consent. Harvesting without permission is extractive and exploitative.  

**Movement principle:**  
Always ask before you take.

---

## Additional Principles
- **Human dignity first:** Technology should never override autonomy, consent, or lived experience.  
- **Transparency:** Communities deserve to know when AI is used and how.  
- **Equity:** AI must not reinforce existing inequalities or replicate oppressive systems.  
- **Care over scale:** Growth is meaningless if it sacrifices trust, safety, or solidarity.  

---

### Closing Note
AI can accelerate processes, but movements are built on **human connection, accountability, and trust**. These values cannot—and should not—be automated.
