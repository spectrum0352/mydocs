Risk-Based Decision Making
•	Prioritization: Focus on mitigating high-impact risks.
•	Cost-Benefit Analysis: Weigh the cost of security measures against potential benefits.
•	Risk Acceptance: Accept some level of risk when necessary, with appropriate controls.
By following these strategies, organizations can create a collaborative environment where security and development teams work together to achieve common goals.
