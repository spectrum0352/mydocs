# Defense in depth Architecture

## The Necessity of Defense in Depth

Why a layered security approach is essential for organizations.

## Rising Cyber Threat Landscape

• Cybercrime reports increased by 8% last year.
• Small businesses lose ~\$50,000 per attack.
• Ransomware payments dropped by 35% in 2024 due to better defenses.

## Common Attack Vectors

• Phishing & social engineering
• Malware & ransomware
• Exploiting unpatched vulnerabilities
• Insider threats

## Why a Single Security Layer Isn’t Enough

• Attackers bypass firewalls with phishing.
• Endpoint protection alone can't stop credential theft.
• Network segmentation failure leads to data breaches.

## Defense in Depth Strategy

• **Physical Security:** Surveillance, access control.
• **Technical Security:** Firewalls, encryption, SIEM.
• **Administrative Security:** Policies, awareness training.

## Case Studies & Lessons Learned

• Private schools targeted by cybercriminals.
• AI-driven cyberattacks increased malicious scanning by 1,200%.
• Companies implementing layered security reduced breach impact.

## Conclusion & Best Practices

• Implement multi-layered security controls.
• Regular security assessments & patching.
• Adopt Zero Trust & AI-driven threat detection.
