NAS and SAN
Storage Area Networks and Network-Attached Storage both provide networked storage solutions. A NAS is a single
storage device that operates on data files, while a SAN is a local network of several devices.
Network Attached Storage (NAS)
Runs over a standard network
Shows up as normal shares on network
Good for small environments
File-level
Storage Area Networks (SAN)
SAN runs on block-level storage
Fibre Channel (FC) or iSCSI
Expensive implementation
