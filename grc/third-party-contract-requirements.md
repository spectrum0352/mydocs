# Information Security Contractual Requirements Checklist for Third-Party Suppliers

# Overview

This checklist provides the minimum contractual information security, privacy, regulatory, operational, and governance requirements that should be included in agreements with third-party suppliers, vendors, managed service providers (MSPs), cloud service providers (CSPs), consultants, contractors, outsourcing partners, and software providers.

The objective is to ensure suppliers adequately protect organizational information assets throughout the entire lifecycle of the contractual relationship.

This checklist aligns with:

* ISO/IEC 27001:2022
* ISO/IEC 27002:2022
* ISO/IEC 27036-2:2022
* GDPR
* DORA
* NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
* NIST SP 800-53
* CIS Controls v8
* SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria
* PCI DSS (where applicable)

## Document Objective

This checklist should be used during:

* Supplier onboarding
* Procurement reviews
* Contract negotiations
* Third-party risk assessments
* Vendor due diligence
* Information security reviews
* Annual supplier reassessments
* Contract renewals
* Regulatory and compliance audits
* Cloud service provider evaluations

It serves as a comprehensive baseline to ensure third-party contracts include the necessary legal, security, privacy, operational, and compliance controls to protect organizational information assets and manage third-party cyber risk throughout the supplier lifecycle.
