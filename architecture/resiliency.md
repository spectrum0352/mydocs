System Resiliency
Resilient systems don’t eliminate risk. Resilient systems fight off attacks more readily than systems with less
resilience. They handle risks better, in other words. The principles here apply to individual systems and to larger
systems.
Generally they handle risks better, adding technologies and processes to enable the system recovery easily.
