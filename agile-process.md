Agile / Scum process

Agile is an iterative and incremental development focused on delivering value quickly through collaboration and flexibility.

What is agile process
The core cycle in Agile development is the Sprint.

Sprint Duration
•	Typically lasts 2 to 4 weeks.
•	The duration is chosen based on the project's complexity and team size.

Sprint Activities and Steps
1.	Sprint Planning:
o	The team defines the work to be done in the sprint.
o	Items are selected from the product backlog and broken down into tasks.
o	Sprint goals are established.
2.	Daily Scrum:
o	A short daily meeting (usually 15 minutes) where the team discusses progress, challenges, and plans for the day.
o	Helps maintain focus and transparency.
3.	Sprint Development:
o	Team members work on the tasks defined in the sprint planning.
o	Development, testing, and integration occur during this phase.
4.	Sprint Review:
o	The team demonstrates the completed work to stakeholders.
o	Feedback is gathered and incorporated into future sprints.
5.	Sprint Retrospective:
o	The team reflects on the sprint, identifying what went well, what did not, and areas for improvement.
o	Lessons learned are applied to future sprints.

Additional Notes:
•	Product Backlog: A prioritized list of requirements or features for the product.
•	Sprint Backlog: A subset of the product backlog selected for development in a specific sprint.
•	Increment: A shippable product or feature produced at the end of each sprint.
Agile is iterative, meaning the process repeats with each sprint, allowing for continuous improvement and adaptation to changing requirements.
